What is Body Type (Prakriti)?

Body type means your natural physical and mental tendencies. You don’t need exact knowledge — just choose what feels closest to you.

Quick help:

  • Vata: Light body, anxiety, dry skin, disturbed sleep
  • Pitta: Acidity, heat, sweating, anger
  • Kapha: Weight gain, tiredness, sleepiness
