About the Project

What is Heal Veda?

Heal Veda is an AI-driven wellness safety platform that helps users make informed decisions when combining Ayurvedic remedies with modern pharmaceutical medicines. The system focuses on preventive care by identifying unsafe combinations and explaining their impact on the body using Ayurvedic principles.

Problem We Address

Many people use Ayurvedic products along with allopathic medicines without understanding their interactions. This can lead to side effects, reduced effectiveness, or long-term imbalance. Existing tools rarely consider Ayurvedic body types and lifestyle factors.

What Makes Heal Veda Unique?

  • Combines Ayurveda and pharmaceutical safety
  • Considers body constitution (Vata, Pitta, Kapha)
  • Focuses on wellness risk instead of diagnosis
  • Provides clear, explainable guidance
  • Supports preventive and responsible self-care

How Heal Veda Works

  1. User enters medicines, herbs, and basic health details
  2. The system evaluates possible interactions
  3. Ayurvedic logic and AI-based reasoning are applied
  4. A Wellness Risk Index is generated
  5. Preventive guidance is shown to the user

Disclaimer

Heal Veda is designed for educational and preventive purposes only. It does not replace professional medical consultation. Users are advised to consult healthcare professionals for serious medical conditions.
