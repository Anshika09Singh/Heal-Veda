🌱 What is Heal Veda?

Heal Veda is an AI-powered wellness safety platform designed to help people safely use Ayurvedic remedies alongside modern medicines. It explains possible interactions, timing, and precautions in a clear and friendly way — without giving medical diagnoses.

⚠️ The Problem We Address

Many people combine herbal products with allopathic medicines without knowing whether they are safe together. This can reduce effectiveness or cause unwanted side effects. Most existing tools ignore Ayurvedic body types and lifestyle factors — Heal Veda bridges this gap.

✨ What Makes Heal Veda Special?

  • ✅ Combines Ayurveda with modern medicine safety
  • ✅ Considers body types (Vata, Pitta, Kapha)
  • ✅ Explains results in simple, non-medical language
  • ✅ Uses AI + OCR to read prescriptions
  • ✅ Focuses on prevention, not diagnosis
  • ✅ Designed for everyday users

🔍 How Heal Veda Works

  1. You upload a prescription or enter medicine details
  2. The system reads and understands the content using AI
  3. Safety rules and Ayurvedic logic are applied
  4. You receive a clear wellness safety explanation
  5. Helpful guidance is shown for safer daily use
